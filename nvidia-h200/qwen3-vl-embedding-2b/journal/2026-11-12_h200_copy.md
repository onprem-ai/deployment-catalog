# 2026-11-12 - Copy for One H200

**Goal:** The official catalog's RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell card (`nvidia-rtx-pro-6000-blackwell/qwen3-vl-embedding-2b`)
as a card for one H200, then a first deployment.

## Changes from the Blackwell card

- `hardware.gpu.product: nvidia-h200`; the format's v1.1.0 schema.
- **Weights:** the Blackwell card reads an onprem-ai mirror (`Qwen--Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B--model`)
  that lists exactly the files of Qwen's own repository. This card takes them from the publisher,
  `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B` at `9f2f7e710d6d81056aa5c0a4f04764fec6bb7bda` (Apache-2.0), so the
  revision is pinned.
- An engine booking of 8 GiB (`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.0572`) and a hardware booking of 10 GiB.
  A first try with 6 GiB of engine booking failed at start-up: "To serve at least one request with
  the models's max seq len (8192), 0.88 GiB KV cache is needed, which is larger than the available
  KV cache memory (0.15 GiB)". The Blackwell card's share of 0.08 gave the engine 7.7 GiB, and the
  vision encoder's profiling takes most of it.
- Everything else as on Blackwell: `--runner pooling --convert embed`, BF16, remote code, 8,192
  tokens, NVIDIA's `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.02-py3` image (vLLM 0.15.1).

## First deployment

Deployed on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), next to a Nemotron 3.5 Super VL
replica on the same GPU. With the 8 GiB engine booking it was ready 40 s after the deploy, and the
replica held 7.5 GiB, within the 10 GiB hardware booking.

Through the API gateway (`POST /v1/embeddings`, no instruction prefix; the model wraps every input
in "Represent the user's input." by default):

- Text: one query and three texts in 0.30 s, 58 input tokens, vectors of 2,048 values with length
  1.0. Cosine similarity to "What causes the northern lights?": the English answer 0.91, the German
  answer 0.79, "Zurich is the largest city in Switzerland." 0.20: ranked right.
- Image: a rendered page (a quarterly report with a revenue table) sent as a chat-style
  `messages` input with one `image_url`: an embedding came back; its similarity to "A quarterly
  business report with a revenue table" was 0.20, to "A photo of a cat sleeping on a sofa" -0.05.
- `dimensions` is refused ("does not support matryoshka representation"): Qwen trains the model for
  64 to 2,048 dimensions, but its `config.json` does not declare it.


## Measured use under load (2026-11-12)

Alone on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), with a load probe on the node sending
straight to the engine: unique texts (no cache hits), 10 s of warm-up, then a 25 s window per level;
the GPU's memory read with `nvidia-smi` every 0.5 s. Short: 32 texts of about 80 tokens per request;
medium: 8 texts of about 500 tokens; longest: one text near the card's context length.

GPU memory: 7,699 MiB right after start, **8,067 MiB at the peak** (7.88 GiB), within the
10 GiB hardware booking. The memory does not grow with load: the engine reserves its KV cache at
start.

## Bookings from the measurements (2026-11-12)

- GPU: engine 8 GiB, hardware 9 GiB; engine booking about the measured use (fixed memory plus a KV cache for about two
  full-length requests), hardware booking about 1 GiB above the measured peak.
- RAM: the container used at most 3.1 GiB under load; reservation 4G, limit 8G, and
  `hardware.memory.host_bytes` set to the limit.
- CPU: the container used at most 2.9 cores under load (full saturation); reservation 2 cores
  (all six H200 embedding cards together reserve 12 of the VM's 16 vCPUs), limit 4.
