# 2026-11-13 - Bring-up and Tuning on H200

Probe: the skill probe on the node, straight to the engine, thinking off; ~245-token prompts with
512-token answers; ~5,300-token prompts with 64-token answers for prefill; 20 s warm-up, 40 s window.
Each round is committed, deployed fresh and undeployed.

## Bring-up

- First start failed: `--quantization fp8_per_block` also quantized the vision encoder, and its first
  run during start-up profiling failed in FlashInfer's block-scaled FP8 kernel
  ("per_token_group_quant_8bit not implemented for Float8_e4m3fn"). Switching video off did not help.
- Fix: `--quantization-config '{"ignore":["*vision_tower*","*audio_tower*","*embed_vision*","*embed_audio*"]}'`:
  the encoders stay BF16, as in Red Hat's FP8 builds of the larger Gemma 4 models. Video stays off.
- Start-up takes 10.6 minutes (`init engine ... took 637.88 s`), mostly JIT compilation for the online
  FP8 kernels; the container loses it on every redeploy.

Round 0: `Model loading took 11.53 GiB`, KV cache 3.98 GiB; the GPU held 29.8 GiB, 9.8 GiB above the
engine booking (CUDA graphs 1.41 GiB and buffers outside the share), above the 24 GiB hardware booking.

Round 1: 28 GiB of engine booking triples the KV cache (958,940 tokens, 7.3 requests of 131,072) and
leaves it a third full at 256 short requests. Throughput with short prompts does not change (11,952
against 11,801 tokens/s at 256): the replica is bound by compute there. The extra room serves long
requests, which the full benchmark's mixed load sends; the 31B card's benchmark showed what a full
KV cache costs there (4.8 s to the first token).

## Chosen

Round 1: 1 draft token, 256 sequences, 16,384 batched tokens, FP8 at load with BF16 encoders, engine
booking 28 GiB (`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.2003`). The GPU held 37.1 GiB (37,999 MiB): hardware booking
39 GiB. The tuning budget (4 h for the 26B, 31B and E4B cards together) ended here; not tried: draft
length 2, no drafter, a different number of sequences. Container: RAM at most 6.3 GiB, CPU at most
2.0 cores: RAM 8 GiB reserved, 12 GiB limit; CPU 2 reserved, 4 limit.
