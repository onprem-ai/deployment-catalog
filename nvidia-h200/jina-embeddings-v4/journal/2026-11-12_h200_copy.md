# 2026-11-12 - Copy for One H200

**Goal:** The official catalog's RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell card (`nvidia-rtx-pro-6000-blackwell/jina-embeddings-v4`)
as a card for one H200, then a first deployment.

## Changes from the Blackwell card

- `hardware.gpu.product: nvidia-h200`; the format's v1.1.0 schema.
- An engine booking of 12 GiB (`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.0858` on the H200's 139.8 GiB)
  inside the Blackwell card's 14 GiB hardware booking, so the profile states what the engine gets.
- Everything else as the Blackwell card has it: `jinaai/jina-embeddings-v4-vllm-retrieval` at
  `1c64a04189fcd31b44753fc8decbf3837766c52e` (the retrieval adapter merged into Qwen2.5-VL-3B, 7.0
  GiB), FP16, `--pooler-config '{"pooling_type": "ALL", "normalize": false}'`, 8,192 tokens, up to
  4 images per request, NVIDIA's `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.02-py3` image (vLLM 0.15.1).

The model's own journal on the Blackwell card names the license: Qwen Research License,
non-commercial; commercial use needs a license from Alibaba Cloud.

## First deployment

Deployed on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), on the same GPU as a Nemotron 3.5
Super VL replica (120.7 GiB) and an EmbeddingGemma 2 replica. Ready 50 s after the deploy.

- Image as on Blackwell (vLLM 0.15.1 from NVIDIA): no change was needed for the H200.
  `Model loading took 7.16 GiB`, `Available KV cache memory: 2.47 GiB`,
  `GPU KV cache size: 71,968 tokens`, `init engine ... took 23.12 seconds`; the replica held
  10.9 GiB, within the 14 GiB hardware booking.
- vLLM 0.15.1 warns that `normalize` in `--pooler-config` is deprecated (vLLM 0.33 refuses it and
  asks for `use_activation`): a move to a newer image must change the pooler config.
- Through the API gateway (`POST /v1/embeddings`, the model card's `Query: ` and `Passage: `
  prefixes): one query and three texts in 0.25 s, 63 input tokens, vectors of 2,048 values, not
  normalized (length 79.7; the pooler config asks for that, so clients compute cosine similarity
  themselves). Cosine similarity to "What causes the northern lights?": the English answer 0.72, the
  German answer 0.63, "Zurich is the largest city in Switzerland." 0.22: ranked right.
- `dimensions` is refused ("does not support matryoshka representation"): the model is trained for
  Matryoshka truncation, but its `config.json` does not say so. A client truncates the 2,048 values
  itself; `--hf-overrides '{"matryoshka_dimensions": [...]}'` would let vLLM do it.


## Measured use under load (2026-11-12)

Alone on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), with a load probe on the node sending
straight to the engine: unique texts (no cache hits), 10 s of warm-up, then a 25 s window per level;
the GPU's memory read with `nvidia-smi` every 0.5 s. Short: 32 texts of about 80 tokens per request;
medium: 8 texts of about 500 tokens; longest: one text near the card's context length.

GPU memory: 11,145 MiB right after start, **11,747 MiB at the peak** (11.47 GiB), within the
14 GiB hardware booking. The memory does not grow with load: the engine reserves its KV cache at
start.

## Bookings from the measurements (2026-11-12)

- GPU: engine 11 GiB, hardware 12 GiB; engine booking about the measured use (fixed memory plus a KV cache for about two
  full-length requests), hardware booking about 1 GiB above the measured peak.
- RAM: the container used at most 3.0 GiB under load; reservation 4G, limit 8G, and
  `hardware.memory.host_bytes` set to the limit.
- CPU: the container used at most 2.3 cores under load (full saturation); reservation 2 cores
  (all six H200 embedding cards together reserve 12 of the VM's 16 vCPUs), limit 4.
