# 2026-11-12 - Copy for One H200

**Goal:** The official catalog's RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell card (`nvidia-rtx-pro-6000-blackwell/jina-v5-text-small`)
as a card for one H200, then a first deployment.

## Changes from the Blackwell card

- `hardware.gpu.product: nvidia-h200`; the format's v1.1.0 schema.
- An engine booking of 6 GiB (`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.0429` on the H200's 139.8 GiB) inside
  the Blackwell card's 8 GiB hardware booking.
- Everything else as the Blackwell card has it: `jinaai/jina-embeddings-v5-text-small-retrieval`
  at `6856e76bb72982e58de0620458a4e8b3614da340`, FP16, last-token pooling with normalization,
  32,768 tokens, prefix caching, NVIDIA's `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.02-py3` image (vLLM 0.15.1).

License as on the Blackwell card: CC-BY-NC-4.0, non-commercial.

## First deployment

Deployed on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), next to a Nemotron 3.5 Super VL
replica on the same GPU. Ready 60 s after the deploy, with no change for the H200.

- `Model loading took 1.12 GiB`, `Available KV cache memory: 4.44 GiB`,
  `GPU KV cache size: 41,552 tokens`, `init engine ... took 20.03 seconds`; the replica held
  6.65 GiB, within the 8 GiB hardware booking.
- Through the API gateway (the model card's `Query: ` and `Document: ` prefixes): one query and
  three texts in 0.20 s, 60 input tokens, vectors of 1,024 values with length 1.0. Cosine
  similarity to "What causes the northern lights?": the English answer 0.87, the German answer
  0.67, "Zurich is the largest city in Switzerland." 0.03: ranked right.
- `dimensions` is refused ("does not support matryoshka representation"): Jina trains the model for
  Matryoshka truncation from 1,024 down to 32, but its `config.json` does not declare it. A client
  truncates and renormalizes the vector itself.


## Measured use under load (2026-11-12)

Alone on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), with a load probe on the node sending
straight to the engine: unique texts (no cache hits), 10 s of warm-up, then a 25 s window per level;
the GPU's memory read with `nvidia-smi` every 0.5 s. Short: 32 texts of about 80 tokens per request;
medium: 8 texts of about 500 tokens; longest: one text near the card's context length.

GPU memory: 6,821 MiB right after start, **7,031 MiB at the peak** (6.87 GiB), within the
8 GiB hardware booking. The memory does not grow with load: the engine reserves its KV cache at
start.

## Bookings from the measurements (2026-11-12)

- GPU: engine 6 GiB, hardware 8 GiB (unchanged); engine booking about the measured use (fixed memory plus a KV cache for about two
  full-length requests), hardware booking about 1 GiB above the measured peak.
- RAM: the container used at most 3.0 GiB under load; reservation 4G, limit 6G, and
  `hardware.memory.host_bytes` set to the limit.
- CPU: the container used at most 2.1 cores under load (full saturation); reservation 2 cores
  (all six H200 embedding cards together reserve 12 of the VM's 16 vCPUs), limit 4.
