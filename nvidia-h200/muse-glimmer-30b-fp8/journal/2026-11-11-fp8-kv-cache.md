# 2026-11-11 - FP8 KV Cache

**Goal:** Check whether the KV cache and the DFlash drafter can run in FP8 instead of BF16,
following the platform's precision rule (FP8 by default, 16-bit only for a recorded reason).
Test service on inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j` (one H200), vLLM v0.33.0.

## Why the card was BF16

No reason was recorded: the compose file set no `--kv-cache-dtype`, so vLLM kept the model's
BF16, and the drafter loaded as published. Neither Meta's model card nor vLLM's recipe for
Muse Glimmer recommends a 16-bit KV cache or drafter.

## Measured

Answers with FP8 KV cache and FP8 drafter: 40 of 40 short questions with known answers
(arithmetic and Python, 4 repeats), and a hidden fact found at 25K and 94K prompt tokens.

Full replica benchmarks (decode tokens/s, `max-decode/1.0.0`; ITL p50 at 1 request):

| KV cache, drafter | 1 request | ITL p50 | 8 | 32 | 64 | 80 | KV cache tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BF16, BF16 (previous card, 2026-11-10) | 323 | 2.93 ms | 2,220 | 4,779 | 5,438 | 5,598 | 3,071,550 |
| FP8, FP8 (job `3bc48669`) | 307 | 3.17 ms | 2,342 | 4,868 | 5,590 | 5,855 | 6,354,188 |
| FP8, BF16 (this card, job `8833e79f`) | 308 | 3.14 ms | 2,138 | 4,736 | 5,489 | 5,689 | 6,140,837 |

The FP8 KV cache costs one user about 5% (3.14 against 2.93 ms per token) and doubles the
KV cache; throughput stays within 3% up to 80 requests. The drafter's precision makes no
measurable difference, so it stays BF16. A short probe beforehand had shown one user 13%
faster with FP8; the full benchmark did not confirm it, so the probe's single-user figure was
noise or the shape of its workload.

Chosen: FP8 KV cache, BF16 drafter, for twice the concurrent long-context requests at a small
single-user cost.

## GPU memory

Peak 121,194,414,080 bytes (112.87 GiB), `vram_used_bytes` every 30 s during the benchmark.
From the deployment log: `Model loading took 37.28 GiB` (target and drafter; the drafter is
its `model.safetensors`, 5,111,976,608 bytes, loaded as is), `CUDA graph pool memory: 1.64
GiB (actual)`, `Available KV cache memory: 68.69 GiB`, `GPU KV cache size: 6,140,837
tokens`, engine budget `(0.8011, 111.99 GiB)`. Runtime overhead is the peak minus the other
parts: 5.26 GiB. Headroom to the 120 GiB hardware booking: 7.13 GiB.
