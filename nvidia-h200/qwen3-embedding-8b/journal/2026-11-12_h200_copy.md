# 2026-11-12 - Copy for One H200

**Goal:** The official catalog's RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell card (`nvidia-rtx-pro-6000-blackwell/qwen3-embedding-8b`)
as a card for one H200, then a first deployment.

## Changes from the Blackwell card

- `hardware.gpu.product: nvidia-h200`; the format's v1.1.0 schema.
- **Weights:** the Blackwell card reads an onprem-ai mirror (`Qwen--Qwen3-Embedding-8B--model`) that
  lists exactly the files of Qwen's own repository. This card takes them from the publisher,
  `Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-8B` at `1d8ad4ca9b3dd8059ad90a75d4983776a23d44af` (Apache-2.0, 14.1 GiB), so
  the revision is pinned.
- An engine booking of 24 GiB (`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.1717`) and a hardware booking of 26 GiB.
  The Blackwell card's share of 0.29 gave the engine 27.5 GiB of its 95 GiB; its journal measured
  19.9 GiB in use.
- Everything else as on Blackwell: BF16, last-token pooling from the checkpoint, 32,768 tokens,
  prefix caching, NVIDIA's `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.02-py3` image (vLLM 0.15.1).

## First deployment

Deployed alone on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`; the Nemotron 3.5 Super VL
service there was undeployed to make room). Ready 60 s after the deploy, with no change for the
H200.

- `Model loading took 14.11 GiB`, `Available KV cache memory: 8.53 GiB`,
  `GPU KV cache size: 62,096 tokens`, `init engine ... took 22.38 seconds`; the replica held
  23.8 GiB, within the 26 GiB hardware booking.
- Through the API gateway (`POST /v1/embeddings`, the model card's instruction for queries,
  "Instruct: Given a web search query, retrieve relevant passages that answer the query\nQuery:",
  and no prefix for documents): one query and three texts in 0.33 s, 77 input tokens, vectors of
  4,096 values with length 1.0. Cosine similarity to "What causes the northern lights?": the English
  answer 0.68, the German answer 0.55, "Zurich is the largest city in Switzerland." 0.07: ranked
  right.
- `dimensions` is refused ("does not support matryoshka representation"): Qwen trains the model for
  32 to 4,096 dimensions, but its `config.json` does not declare it.


## Measured use under load (2026-11-12)

Alone on one H200 (inference node `nebius-e00pn5kkfnayh8wn6j`), with a load probe on the node sending
straight to the engine: unique texts (no cache hits), 10 s of warm-up, then a 25 s window per level;
the GPU's memory read with `nvidia-smi` every 0.5 s. Short: 32 texts of about 80 tokens per request;
medium: 8 texts of about 500 tokens; longest: one text near the card's context length.

GPU memory: 24,977 MiB right after start, **24,977 MiB at the peak** (24.39 GiB), within the
26 GiB hardware booking. The memory does not grow with load: the engine reserves its KV cache at
start.

## Bookings from the measurements (2026-11-12)

- GPU: engine 22 GiB, hardware 24 GiB; engine booking about the measured use (fixed memory plus a KV cache for about two
  full-length requests), hardware booking about 1 GiB above the measured peak.
- RAM: the container used at most 3.0 GiB under load; reservation 4G, limit 6G, and
  `hardware.memory.host_bytes` set to the limit.
- CPU: the container used at most 2.0 cores under load (full saturation); reservation 2 cores
  (all six H200 embedding cards together reserve 12 of the VM's 16 vCPUs), limit 4.
